Emberwake — Hold the Dawn Line: an original top-down arena shooter. Pilot a lantern skiff with WASD/arrow keys, aim with the mouse, hold click or Space to fire, Shift to dash (briefly invulnerable), and hold the dawn ember through 6 waves of twilight husks — clear wave 6 to win, lose your hull and the dawn goes out. Three enemy kinds, combo multiplier, pickups, wave scaling, particles and screen shake; all music and sound are synthesized live with WebAudio. Plain static files, fully offline, no libraries.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts && node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 to play.
